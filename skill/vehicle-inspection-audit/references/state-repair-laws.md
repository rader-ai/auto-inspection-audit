# State Auto Repair Laws — 50 States + DC

**As of 2026-10-20.** Estimate, authorization, invoice, parts-return and records rules for auto repair shops, by state. Paraphrased, not legal advice. Dollar and percentage figures are exact as read. Thresholds change (FL 2024, MI 2025–26, OH 2026), so confirm anything consequential against current official text or the regulator.

**Markers:** unmarked = read in primary or near-primary text (legislature, agency, Cornell LII, or public.law mirror). **[secondary source]** = news, legal aid or aggregator only. **[UNVERIFIED]** = not confirmed; for "UDAP only" states this includes the *absence* of a repair-specific rule (no exhaustive search was run). Treat unverified items as leads.

California is covered in depth in `california-bar.md`; it gets only a summary row here.

## 1) Which state's rules apply

1. **The shop's location controls,** not the customer's home state. Every statute here regulates repair facilities operating in that state. A Florida resident repaired in Georgia is under Georgia law (UDAP only).
2. **Mobile or remote work:** use the state where the work was performed (FL covers mobile shops; HI covers mobile facilities under the dealer's registration; CA requires separate mobile ARD registration outside a set radius).
3. **Check scope exclusions before applying a rule:**
   - CO: excludes trucks > 8,500 lb GVW, farm tractors, motorcycles/autocycles.
   - IA: excludes vehicles > 12,000 lb GVWR; covers farm/personal-use vehicles only.
   - IL: excludes collision shops (covered by 815 ILCS 308), fleet work (2+ vehicles), firm-price tire/battery/oil installs.
   - MN: excludes jobs ≥ 90% insurer/service-contract paid and free warranty work; estimate rules apply > $100, invoice rules > $50.
   - NC: exempts jobs ≤ $350, government shops, vehicles ≥ 26,001 lb, certain third-party-paid jobs.
   - NH: excludes motor trucks and motorcycles.
   - VA: antique cars exempt. WA: collector/custom vehicles excepted from estimate rule.
   - MA c. 100A registration covers collision and glass shops only.
4. **Date of service matters.** Apply the rule in force when the work was done (e.g., FL threshold $100 → $150 under ch. 2024-137; OH rule amended eff. 3/21/2026; MI 2025 PA 59 eff. 2/1/2026).
5. **Shop type matters.** Ask whether it is a dealer, independent, collision/glass, or fleet shop; collision and fleet work are often under different rules. Commercial vehicles → `commercial-fleet.md`.
6. **No repair statute?** Apply the state's UDAP (unfair/deceptive acts) law and the universal checks in §4. Do not cite thresholds that don't exist.
7. **Regulator to point a consumer to:** CA BAR; FL FDACS; MI Dept. of State; CT DMV Dealers & Repairers; HI Motor Vehicle Repair Industry Board (DCCA); NV DMV; NY DMV; DC DLCP; WI DATCP; everywhere else, the state AG's consumer protection division. Point only; never file for the user. Frame findings as "possible noncompliance," not legal conclusions.

## 3) Quick-calc tables

### 3.1 When is a written estimate required?

| Rule type | States |
|---|---|
| Before any work / any paid work | CA, CT (max-cost est.; not under $50 unless asked), DC, HI, MA (written auth + total price), NJ, OR, CO (written or oral) |
| Above a dollar threshold, automatically | NV > $50; MI ≥ $50; IL > $100; NM > $100; WA > $100; FL > $150; NC > $350 |
| Customer must be offered a choice (written / oral / none / call above $X) | IA > $50; OH > $50; WI > $50 (written repair order); IL, FL, CO waiver forms |
| Only on request | NH (any), NY, RI, VA > $25, MD > $50, MN > $100, TN > $250 (plus duty to disclose the right) |
| No dollar figure, but written/express auth | PA, UT |
| None (UDAP only or general rule) | ID (no est. required), and all "—" rows |

Waivers: nearly every statute requires a **signed written** waiver. If the shop claims one, look for the document.

### 3.2 Overage cushion (how far the bill may exceed the authorized estimate without new consent)

Compute (final − estimate) ÷ estimate, **pre-tax** where specified (IA, OH, WA explicitly).

| Cushion | States |
|---|---|
| **$0** (any increase needs consent) | CA, CT, NJ, NY, RI, WI |
| **$10** | MA |
| **Lesser of 10% or $25** | CO |
| **Lesser of 10% or $50** | MI |
| **Lesser of 10% or $200** | OR |
| **Greater of $10 or 10%, max $50** | FL |
| **Greater of 10% or $50** | NM |
| **10%** | IL, IA, MD, MN, NC (+ authorized increases), NH (written consent), OH, UT, WA, VA (20% if vehicle ≥ 25 model years) |
| **15% (< $100) / 10% (> $100)** | HI |
| **20% (≤ $300) / 10% (> $300)** | DC |
| **Lesser of 20% or $100** (notice + approval) | NV |
| **25%** | TN [secondary source] |
| **Customer-set cap** | PA; WI option (2); IL/IA/FL "call me above $X" choices |
| "Unreasonable" increase | ID |

An overrun beyond the cushion with no recorded authorization is the most common objectively checkable defect. Several states also bar a lien on or holding the car for unauthorized excess (CO, FL, IL, NC, WA, WI, NH loses payment right, NV lien blocked by non-compliant statement).

## 4) Universal checks

Patterns common to most repair statutes. For UDAP-only states, use these as best-practice benchmarks and frame defects as documentation gaps, not violations; the enforceable UDAP themes everywhere are **misrepresenting that work is needed** and **charging for work not performed** (explicit in AK, ID, TX).

| Check | What to look for | States with an explicit rule |
|---|---|---|
| Estimate existed before work | Estimate date/time earlier than repair start or tech clock-in; threshold met | §3.1 |
| Waiver / choice form | Signed waiver or choice (written / no est. / call above $X); cap honored | CO, CT, FL, HI, IL, IA, MA, MI, NC, OH, WI, NJ, NM, NV |
| Final ≤ estimate + cushion | Compute pre-tax | §3.2 |
| Re-authorization documented | For each overage or added repair: date, time, who authorized, phone/email, what was added, new total, employee who took it | CA, CO, CT, IL, MA (exact statement), NJ, NM, NY, OR, PA, RI, WA, WI, DC |
| Digital ("approve via text link") approvals | Should carry equivalent data: timestamp, identity, line items, dollar amount, link to RO number | **Only CA** defines electronic authorization explicitly; OR accepts fax/email attached to the estimate; CT fax/email for one acknowledgment only. Elsewhere unresolved (E-SIGN/UETA likely apply; outside this file) |
| Required vs. suggested labeling | Each recommendation labeled | IL (est. must say "required or suggested") |
| Necessity basis | Each recommendation tied to manufacturer spec, measured condition, or customer request | **IL 306/80 and WA 46.71.045 "reasonable basis"** definitions are the best yardstick. Bans on misrepresenting necessity: AK, CA, CO, CT, FL, HI, IA, ID, MA, MI, NC, OH, PA, TX, UT, WI. MI and MN ban performing known-unnecessary repairs unless the customer insists after being told |
| Reason for each repair on invoice | Each DVI finding supports its line | UT (invoice must state reason) |
| Diagnosis disclosure | After paid diagnosis: malfunction, remedy, **tests used** | MI 257.1307a |
| Diagnostic / teardown fees | Disclosed and agreed before diagnosis; reassembly cost shown | CA, CO, CT (written if ≥ $50), FL, IL, MI, NC, NJ, NV, OR, VA, WI, DC, HI |
| "Dangerous" claims | Safety flags backed by measurements | CO, FL, IA, ID, MA, NC, OH, PA, UT, WI ban false "dangerous" claims |
| Invoice itemization | Parts (name, number, price); labor (hours × rate or flagged flat-rate); fees; tax | All statute states. Part numbers: CO, MA, PA, WA. Separate subtotals: CA, HI, IL. Actual vs flat-rate: MA, WI |
| Used / rebuilt / non-OEM flag | Each non-new part flagged; new parts OEM or not | All statute states flag used/rebuilt. OEM vs non-OEM: MN, CA (crash), NY (body), WA (body). Prior written disclosure: MI. Aftermarket crash parts: HI (signed acceptance), IA, NM, UT (HB 119) |
| Who did the work | Tech name or ID on invoice | CO, IA, MI, OH, UT, WI |
| Odometer recorded | On WO or invoice | CA, CO, CT, FL, IL, MA, MN, NJ, NY, PA, RI (in/out), WA, DC, HI (WO) |
| Customer's complaint recorded | Symptoms/requested repairs on WO | CA, CT, FL, MA, MN (invoice), HI, MI, DC |
| Replaced parts | Return/inspection offered; request timing | **Must tender:** IA, MD, MI. **Must offer:** OH, VA. **On request at/before auth:** CA, CO, CT, FL, HI, MN, NH, NJ, NY (written), NC, RI (written), WA, WI. **On request:** ID, NV. **Disclosed right:** MA, PA, UT. **None:** IL. Warranty/core/exchange parts usually exempt from return but open to inspection. Failure to produce requested parts is a strong red flag |
| Shop credentials | Registration/license number on documents; current status | CA (ARD # on invoice and ads), FL (FDACS # on invoice), MI (all documents), HI, CT, NV (lien depends on it), NY, DC |
| Records lookback | Request RO, estimate, auth log, parts purchase invoices within period | CA 3 yr; CO 3 yr; HI 2 yr; IL 2 yr; NY 2 yr; WI 2 yr; FL 12 mo; MN 1 yr; NH 1 yr; NV 1 yr; OR 1 yr; WA 1 yr; IA 9 mo. Parts **purchase** invoices (CA, NY, WI) confirm a part was bought |

### Arizona — UDAP only
Consumer Fraud Act, A.R.S. 44-1521 et seq. (44-1522: deception, misrepresentation, concealment of material facts).

### Arkansas — UDAP only [UNVERIFIED]
Deceptive Trade Practices Act, Ark. Code 4-88-101 et seq. (4-88-107). Not read online.

### California — see `california-bar.md`
BAR registers ARDs; written estimate and recorded authorization (written / oral / electronic) before any work; revised estimate with new total before added work; plain-language line items; no "shop supplies"; parts presumed new unless labeled; 3-year records with unique transaction ID; ball-joint and A/C invoice measurements; Smog Check/STAR/CAP; VSSI (replaced Brake & Lamp, Sept 27, 2024). Necessity hooks: B&P §9884.7(a)(1),(4); CLRA §1770(a)(15).

### Colorado — C.R.S. 42-9-101 et seq.
- Scope: excludes trucks > 8,500 lb GVW, farm tractors, motorcycles/autocycles; washing and adding gas/oil aren't repairs (42-9-102).
- Estimate (42-9-104): written consent generally required; estimate includes expected completion date; copy to customer. Towed/after-hours deemed waiver capped at **$100**. Waiver by prescribed bold statement doesn't itself authorize repairs. Disassembly → estimate shows reassembly cost.
- Overage (42-9-106): paying the excess isn't consent; no lien for unconsented excess; must release once estimate + margin paid.
- Invoice (42-9-108): each mechanic's name/employee # and stage handled; subcontracted work identified; flat-rate exceptions.
- Parts (42-9-109): exceptions for body-shop, warranty/exchange parts; airbags may not be returned.
- Prohibited (42-9-111): unconsented repairs; claiming necessity when not a fact; claiming work done when not; falsely calling vehicle/part dangerous; emissions repairs the failure doesn't indicate; no invoice or no odometer.
- Enforcement: deceptive trade practice under CCPA (42-9-112) [UNVERIFIED wording]. Source is an unofficial mirror current through fall 2025.

## 6) Conflicts and open questions

**Conflicts between or within sources (not resolved here):**
- **Ohio threshold:** AG business guide says $25; authenticated rule text (eff. 3/21/2026) says $50. This file uses $50.
- **$50 boundary wording:** CT and MI text says the estimate is "not required under $50"; the source checklists render this as CT "> $50" and MI "≥ $50". At exactly $50, treat as required for MI and check text for CT.
- **E-authorization:** the A–M research concluded no statute addresses text/e-signature approvals except CT (one acknowledgment). The N–W research found OR accepts fax/email, and CA defines electronic authorization explicitly. The table reflects all three; elsewhere treat as unresolved.
- **New Mexico overage:** "greater of 10% or $50" seen only on the state-hosted copy, not the Justia copy.

**Open questions:** Maine AG rule existence; MI 2025 PA 59 exact §32 edits, HB 5149, retention; MN 2024 c 114 changes; FL $150 effective date and county licensing; CT, MA, NJ, DC record retention; CO registration and 42-9-112 wording; MD county registration; TN official text and invoice/parts/records rules; OR 2026 ch. 646A changes; UT HB 119 codified text; VA repealed §59.1-207.5:1 subject; DC invoice provisions; all UDAP-only "absence" claims (AL, AR, DE, GA, IN, KY, LA, MS, MO, KS, AZ, MT, ND, SD, WY, VT, WV, OK, TX) not exhaustively searched.
