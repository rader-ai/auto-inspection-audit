## 1) Agencies and law map

| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **Bureau of Automotive Repair (BAR)** | Inside Dept. of Consumer Affairs (DCA). Registers Automotive Repair Dealers (ARDs); licenses Smog Check stations/inspectors/repair techs and VSSI stations/techs; citation system; undercover enforcement. Authorizing section (§9882) **sunsets Jan 1, 2028**. Informal 3-member citation conference panel (Bureau, public, industry) since Jul 1, 2023 |

## 2) Registration, coverage, signs, ads

- **ARD** = anyone who for compensation diagnoses or repairs vehicle malfunctions, or collects payment for repairs it refers or sublets (§9880.1(a)). Referral and sublet brokers must register (rule eff. Oct 1, 2025).
- **Repair** = all maintenance and repair by an ARD, incl. body work. Excluded: pre-arranged *commercial business agreements* (fleet; warranties/service plans sold to the public don't count) and *roadside services* (§9880.1(e),(k),(l)). Warranty work is presumed compensated (§3303(g)).
- **"Preventative maintenance"** (§9880.1(j)): tire pressure/rotation, fluid and filter changes, belts, wipers, bulbs and headlamp restoration, fuel-induction service, additives through fill points, top-offs, plus related R&R. These can skip a written estimate (§9884.9(e); see §3).
- **"Customer"** = person who presents the vehicle and authorizes work; never the ARD, its staff, or an insurer on a claim (§9880.1(f)).
- **Registration mandatory and current** (§9884.6). Body shops declare body work and attest to local business license, resale permit, EPA hazardous-waste ID, air-district spray-booth permit (§9889.52). Mobile ARDs need separate mobile registration outside a set radius (eff. Oct 1, 2025).
- **Signs** (§9884.17; §§3351.3–3351.4): official ARD sign visible to customers with BAR phone/website and notice that replaced parts may be requested **when the WO is written**. Licenses displayed under glass. Mobile ARDs give a paper sign copy with each WO and show name, ARD #, phone in 72-point type on the vehicle. Smog stations post "Notice to Consumers" (H&S §44017.3; §3340.22.2); STAR and VSSI stations post their own signs.
- **Online ads** (§3371.2, eff. Oct 1, 2025): every internet ad shows BAR-registered business name, ARD #, phone (per location, or link to a site that does). Ads/signs use the registered name and address (§3371).

## 3) Estimates

1. **Written estimate before any work or charges** for a *specific job*; no work and no charges until customer authorizes (§9884.9(a); §3353(a)).
2. **Plain language** (§3352(b), eff. Jul 1, 2025): "specific job" = all repairs in one transaction; each listed in terms a layperson understands, no trade jargon. WIR: no acronyms on estimates or invoices.
3. **Parts presumed new** unless labeled used, rebuilt or reconditioned (§3353(a)).
4. **Body/collision estimates itemized** (§9884.9(c); §3353(b)): labor and parts separate, each part identified. New crash parts must be OEM unless labeled **"non-OEM aftermarket"**; "Opt-OEM" and "Like Kind Quality/LKQ" don't satisfy the rule. Paint and materials by hourly rate or actual cost (methods added to WIR Jan 2026).
5. **Third-party (insurer/warranty) estimates** (§3353(b)(1),(d), eff. Jul 1, 2025; payor defined §3303(w)): tell customer the payor estimate is governed by Fair Claims Settlement Practices Regs; record intent to repair per that estimate, payor name, claim number, payor total; attach payor estimate; notify payor before customer-approved changes that alter payment. List payor-approved amount if known, else notice that customer must contact payor for approval.
6. **Teardown** (§3352(e); §3353(c), amended Jul 1, 2025): teardown is itself a repair. Before teardown the estimate shows teardown cost + general description of area (not every part), reassembly cost, parts normally destroyed (gaskets, seals, O-rings), warning that original condition may not be restorable (where applicable), and a **maximum reassembly time**. After teardown: **second, itemized estimate** and authorization for repair or reassembly; a decline goes on the teardown invoice.
7. **Sublet** (§9884.9(b); §3353(f)): estimate states work that may be done by an outside party; consent required unless customer can't reasonably be notified; ARD stays responsible and names the sublet facility on request. **Smog inspections may not be sublet.**
8. **Towing** (§3353(e), eff. Jul 1, 2025): if ARD provides/arranges a tow, disclose tow fees on a **separate document** from estimate and invoice before taking payment (per Veh. Code §22651.07), with separate authorization.
9. **Preventive-maintenance exemption** (§9884.9(e)): no written estimate for §9880.1(j) services if customer authorizes and the service is free, or its total price is conspicuously posted or shown to and acknowledged by the customer.
10. **Estimate content rules (WIR):** sales tax only on invoice; hazardous/toxic waste fee itemized separately **with ARD's EPA ID number**; **no generic "shop supplies"** — only supplies actually used and shown; core charges OK for exchange parts, not warranty parts; Smog stations post labor rate.
11. **Specialized estimates:**
    - Automatic transmissions (§3361.1): inspect before removal incl. control-module code retrieval on electronic units; tell customer if minor/external service would likely fix it; "rebuilt" etc. only after specified work; "exchanged" paired with new/used/rebuilt; standards can't be used to push a rebuild when the customer wants a cheaper fix.
    - Windshields: OEM or non-OEM stated; drive-away wait warning.
    - **A/C (§3366):** words "service," "inspection," "diagnosis," "top off," or "performance check" trigger a mandatory **16-point inspection**.

## 4) Authorization and revised estimates

- **Definitions** (§3352(f)–(h)): authorization = consent by handwritten signature, or oral or electronic statement approving a specific job. *Oral* = in person, phone, or any channel where voice is heard. *Electronic* = digital, wireless, optical, etc. — includes **texts, email, app approvals**.
- **No diagnosis or repair — even no-charge or warranty work — without specific authorization** (§3353.1(a)).
- **Required record by authorization type** (§3353.1(b)–(d); on the estimate or supplemental documents tied to it):

| Type | Must record |
|---|---|
| Written | Signature and date |
| Oral | Date, time, authorizer's name, phone number called (if a call was placed) |
| Electronic | Date, time, authorizer's name, phone number or email used |

- **Texts and emails are records** (§3353.1(e); §3358(e)): each supplemental document (incl. email/text chains) uniquely identified and kept with the same transaction.
- **Exceeding the estimate** (§3354(a); §9884.9(a)): before any extra diagnosis/repair or any charge above the authorized price, give a **revised estimate** listing added parts and labor, their cost, and the **new total**; obtain and record authorization. Consent must come *after* discovering the estimate is insufficient and *before* the extra work. **Cushion: $0.**
- **Recording oral/electronic increases** (§9884.9(a); §3356(e)): WO records date, time, authorizer, phone/email, added parts and labor, added cost. Invoice repeats this, **or** customer signs/initials the statutory acknowledgment of notice and oral approval.
- **No silent changes** (§3354(b)): once an itemized WO is authorized, repair method or parts can't change without new authorization.
- **Designated person** (§9884.9(d); §3354(c)): at initial authorization customer may name someone to approve added work; required title, designee name and contact, customer signature and date, WO number. **ARD, insurer on the claim, and their agents may never be designee.**
- **After-hours / towed-in ("unusual circumstances")** (§3353.2): estimate prepared, communicated, and authorization documented **before any diagnosis**. A note left with the car is not authorization.
- **Copies** (§9884.7(a)(2)–(3)): customer gets a copy of any signed document immediately. A signed WO omitting requested repairs or odometer reading is a disciplinary ground.

## 5) Invoices and part labels

**Statutory** (§9884.8): all work incl. warranty on an invoice; each service and part described; labor and parts separated with separate subtotals; tax shown; used/rebuilt/reconditioned parts disclosed, incl. component systems mixing new and non-new; crash parts identified OEM or non-OEM aftermarket; one copy to customer, one kept.

**Regulatory** (§3356):
- ARD number plus business name and address **exactly as BAR has them**.
- Every service (incl. diagnosis and warranty work) priced individually.
- Every part priced and labeled **new, used, reconditioned, rebuilt, OEM crash part, or non-OEM aftermarket crash part**. A kit may be one line if brand and part number shown.
- Labor subtotal, parts subtotal, tax, grand total.
- Authorization details for unusual-circumstance and oral/electronic approvals.
- Teardown declines and unreturned parts noted.
- Legible copy for customer.

**Only listed parts can be charged; generic charges banned** (§3356(f)–(g)): no separate "shop supplies," "miscellaneous parts," or smog-database communication fees.

**Specialized invoice requirements:**
| Job | Invoice must show | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Smog | Vehicle Inspection Report (VIR) attached | §3340.41(a) |
| **A/C** | **High- and low-side pressures and center outlet temperature** | §3366 |
| Windshield | Installation date and time, cure time, OEM/non-OEM | WIR |
| Mobile repair | Location of repair (added Jan 2026) | WIR |
| **Ball joints** | **Measured wear of the replaced joint AND manufacturer's maximum allowable wear** | §3360.2 |

## 6) Replaced parts, records, trade standards

- **Return of parts** (§9884.10; §3355): if requested at WO/estimate stage, returned at completion. Exempt: parts too large/heavy under regulation, exchange (core) parts, warranty parts going back to manufacturer. For exempt parts the shop offers to *show* the part, tells customer orally, and notes on WO and invoice that it can't be returned. Nothing to show if part not charged.
- **Records: 3 years** (§9884.11; §3358): invoices incl. **supplier invoices for parts and sublet labor**, estimates with authorization records, WOs with supplemental authorization records. Paper or electronic (scan paper signatures per WIR). Open to BAR during business hours. **All share a unique transaction identifier.**
- **Accepted trade standards (Article 8):** ball joints (wear measured with purpose-built gauge in thousandths of an inch or mm, per maker's spec; §§3360.1–3360.3); automatic transmissions (§3361.1); engine changes (§3362.1); VIN/emissions/recall labels (§3364); auto body, frame, sectioning, corrosion protection (§3365); windshields (§3365.1); A/C (§3366; equipment §3351.6); airbags (§3367; previously deployed airbags banned); towing referral fees (§3368). **No brake-specific Article 8 standard** — repairs must be good and workmanlike per manufacturer procedures.

## 7) Prohibited acts, enforcement, fines

**Grounds for discipline** (§9884.7; acts of ARD *or its technicians/employees*, unless bona fide error):
- (a)(1) untrue or misleading statement the shop knew or with reasonable care should have known was untrue/misleading
- (a)(2) signed WO without requested repairs or odometer
- (a)(3) no copy of signed document
- (a)(4) fraud — incl. intentional **or grossly negligent** misrepresentation, bad-faith promises, intentional concealment (§9884.7(e))
- (a)(5) gross negligence; (a)(6) material noncompliance with Act/regs
- (a)(7) willful material departure from accepted trade standards, to someone's detriment, without owner consent
- (a)(8) false promises likely to induce authorization; (a)(9) unauthorized sublet; (a)(10) Penal Code §551 conviction
- Discipline usually per location; all locations for repeated willful violations (§9884.7(b)–(c)). Remedial training only for documentation/minor violations, never fraud (§9884.7(d)).

**False/misleading records** (§3373): no omission or insertion that makes an estimate, invoice or WO false or misleading. Misleadingness judged by overall impression on the target audience, **including the gullible or uninformed** (§3372). Price ads misleading if bait-and-switch, falsely imply inclusions, **create a false impression that more work is needed**, or omit routine extras (disclosure cures the last; must say extra cost may be substantial if > 25%) (§3372.1).

**Unnecessary repairs in practice:** BAR's main tool is the undercover car (known condition or no defect). Typical charges: (a)(1), (a)(4)/(e), §3373. CLRA §1770(a)(15) separately makes it unlawful to represent that a part or repair is needed when it is not.

**Citations and fines:** order of abatement plus optional fine, cap **$5,000 per inspection/investigation** (§125.9). Factors: seriousness/harm, history, good/bad faith, payment for work not done, restitution/mitigation, intent, negligence, owner/manager involvement, cooperation. Fines due in 30 days; unpaid fines block renewal (§§3394.50–3394.52, eff. Jul 1, 2023). Remedial-training program (keeps citation off BAR website) extended to Jan 1, 2028 by SB 774 (2025). Cost recovery in Accusations under §125.3 (e.g., $22,091 in a 2017 revocation, ARD 241395). Citation response deadlines are short (one practitioner: often 15 days).

**Notable enforcement:**
- **Sears (1992):** unneeded repairs alleged in 34 of ~37–38 undercover runs (reports differ), tied to quotas and commission pay; CA centers on 3-yr probation, ~$46.6M customer coupons nationwide, commission/quota scheme dropped.
- **Midas / 22 shops (~2013):** $1.8M settlement and lifetime BAR licensing ban after ~30 undercover runs, 105 violations incl. bait-and-switch.
- **Firestone (from 2013):** Accusation covering 20+ CA locations; outcome [UNVERIFIED].
- **2024:** Airport Collision RPR & Tow (Inglewood) revoked — unjustified tow charge, work not authorized or performed. Two LA-area registrations revoked for storage-fee abuse.
- **2025:** Hernandez Smog (South Gate) revoked after two undercover runs; passed a vehicle that should have failed, once for a $200 payment (eff. 12/24/2025).
- No 2015–2026 multi-state chain upsell case found on bar.ca.gov [UNVERIFIED whether one exists].

## 8) MPI/DVI under California rules

**No BAR rule or guidance specifically governs courtesy multi-point or digital inspections** [UNVERIFIED that none exists], and **no rule requires DVI photos** [UNVERIFIED]. General rules apply:
- An inspection performed **for a charge** is a diagnosis/repair needing an estimate and authorization (§§9884.9, 3353.1).
- Every finding or recommendation is a **"statement"** under §9884.7(a)(1), (a)(4) and CLRA §1770(a)(15).
- A recommendation converted to a line item must meet estimate, authorization and invoice rules.
- Trigger words have fixed meanings (A/C "service/inspection/performance check" → §3366 16-point check).
- Ball-joint replacement must be backed by measured wear vs spec, recorded on the invoice (§3360.2).
- Photos/videos sent by text or email for approval become supplemental authorization records — keep 3 years with the unique ID (§3353.1(e); §3358).

## 9) Smog Check, STAR, CAP

- **Who needs it** (H&S §44000 et seq.): biennially at renewal; at change of ownership (seller's duty; pass within 90 days for private sales); first CA registration. Covered: gas and hybrid 1976+, diesel 1998+ (≤ 14,000 lb GVWR). Exempt: gas/hybrid newest **8 model years** for renewal (abatement fee instead) and **4 model years** for transfer (transfer fee), 1975 and older, diesel 1997 and older or > 14,000 lb, EVs, motorcycles. Stations set inspection prices; certificate fee **$8.25**; stations must give an estimate and get authorization before testing.
- **Station types:** test-only, test-and-repair, repair-only, **STAR** (voluntary higher-performance certification, judged partly by Follow-up Pass Rate). Vehicles directed to STAR must be tested and retested at STAR stations (§3340.41(e)). STAR rules updated Jul 1, 2022.
- **OBD fail criteria** (§3340.42.2): MIL doesn't light at key-on engine-off, or is on/commanded on while running; stored DTC; no communication; software configuration not matching manufacturer or CARB-exempted configuration; incomplete readiness monitors. **Readiness amendment (adopted Jul 9, 2025, eff. Oct 1, 2025; §3340.42.2(a)(9)):** gas MY1996+ and diesel MY1998+ fail if **any** monitor is "not complete." Zero allowed. The old allowances are deleted: one incomplete for gas MY1996–1999, the EVAP-monitor exclusion for gas MY2000+, and the DPF-monitor exclusion for diesel MY2007+. — https://www.bar.ca.gov/pdf/regulatory-actions/readiness-monitor-limits-2024/approved-text.pdf
- **Repairs** follow manufacturer or nationally recognized procedures (§3340.41(d)); biometric inspector login (palm-vein; device update eff. Jul 1, 2026); VIR attached to invoice (§3340.41(a),(f)).
- **A failure identifies an emissions condition or OBD fault, not a failed part.** Repairs need diagnosis under §3340.41(d), estimate and authorization, and may be covered by emissions warranty. Aftermarket emissions parts need a CARB EO. **Smog Check Referee** handles disputes, unusual vehicles, hard-to-find parts.
- **Consumer Assistance Program (CAP)** (amounts eff. Jan 1, 2025; §§3394.3–3394.4):
  - Repair assistance: household income ≤ 225% of federal poverty level, after a failed biennial test. BAR pays 80%, owner copay ≥ 20%, up to $500; up to **$1,100 (MY 1976–1995)** or **$1,450 (MY 1996+)** if BAR agrees repairs are necessary and cost-effective. Work at a contracted STAR test-and-repair station; diagnosis paid at 1.5× posted hourly rate.
  - Retirement: **$2,000** (income-eligible) or **$1,350** (others).

## 10) VSSI (replaced Brake & Lamp)

- **Status:** Brake & Lamp station and adjuster licensing became **inoperative Sept 27, 2024**. Replaced by the **Vehicle Safety Systems Inspection (VSSI)** program (16 CCR Art. 2.5, §§3311.1–3314.2, adopted Mar 27, 2024). Licensed technicians at licensed stations, using the **VSSI Manual (Sept 2023)**, incorporated by reference. SB 861 (2025) cleaned up statutory cross-references. Any document still referring to a "brake and lamp certificate" for post-Sept-2024 work is outdated.
- **Who needs a VSSI:** DMV registration of revived salvage and junk vehicles, specially constructed vehicles, motorcycles (in that context), trailers ≥ 3,000 lb GVWR, off-highway vehicles converted to on-highway. **Brake and lamp "fix-it" tickets no longer require a certificate** — defect fixed and signed off by a licensed station or law enforcement. CA has **no periodic safety inspection** for ordinary passenger cars.
- **VSSI Do-Not-Certify minimums** (pass/fail floor, **not** replacement recommendations):
  - Brake linings: at or above manufacturer service limit; if none, **≥ 1/32 in bonded** or **1/64 in above rivet heads**.
  - Rotors not below minimum thickness; drums not above maximum diameter; thickness variation, runout, cracks, corrosion checked. Gauges read to 0.001 in (rotors), 0.005 in (drums).
  - Tires: **≥ 2/32 in** passenger/light truck; heavy-duty 4/32 steer, 2/32 other.
  - Also: booster function, warning lights, headlight aim, airbags (no evidence of prior deployment), structural rust and frame damage, OBD scan MY2000+.
  - **Open NHTSA recall fails the inspection.**
  - Station gives written estimate for the inspection (§9884.9; §3353).

## 11) Auto body and insurer overlap

- Auto body shop = ARD location doing body or crash repair (§9889.51). Itemized estimates and crash-part labels (§3 #4; §5). §3365 requires OEM or accepted industry repair specs, sectioning standards, corrosion protection.
- **BAR free collision-repair inspection** for repairs done in CA after the owner bought the vehicle; checks invoiced repairs were actually done.
- **Insurer (Ins. Code §758.5):** may not require a particular shop; may recommend only on request or after written notice of right to choose, and must then restore to pre-loss condition at no extra cost; if policy names a preferred shop, can't cut reasonable costs elsewhere by comparing to preferred-shop pricing. ARD estimates built on insurer estimates follow §3353(b)(1),(d). Insurer can't be customer's designee (§§9880.1(f), 9884.9(d)).

## 12) CARB parts rules

- Aftermarket, add-on and modified emissions parts need a **CARB Executive Order** to be legal on emission-controlled vehicles; EO numbers checked by Smog stations, Referees, CARB lookup tool. Anti-tampering: Veh. Code §27156 bars disconnecting, modifying or altering required emission controls (b) and installing, selling or advertising devices that alter their design or performance (c); CARB may exempt an alteration that doesn't reduce effectiveness (h).
- **Catalytic converters:** a new aftermarket converter may be installed, sold or advertised only with a CARB exemption EO (13 CCR §2222(h)(1)–(3)); otherwise use an OE converter or OEM replacement part. **Used, recycled or salvaged converters may not be installed, sold, offered or advertised** (on or after Jul 1, 2008; §2222(i)(1)). Converters under rescinded or withdrawn EOs can't be installed or sold. EOs usually limit vehicles and model years, and many allow installation only after the OEM converter warranty expires; check the EO for the vehicle. — https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/13-CCR-2222 ; https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/aftermarket-catalytic-converter-database
- **Clean Truck Check (HD I/M):** CARB program for heavy-duty non-gasoline vehicles operating in CA (incl. out-of-state); annual compliance fee and periodic testing by CARB-credentialed testers; CARB notice (Mar 10, 2026) says requirements in effect. > 14,000 lb GVWR threshold, semiannual frequency, fee amount [UNVERIFIED]. See also `federal.md`, `commercial-fleet.md`.

## 13) Other California laws

- **SB 478 all-in pricing** (CLRA §1770(a)(29), operative Jul 1, 2024): unlawful to advertise/display a price omitting mandatory fees; only government taxes/fees and actual shipping may be excluded. *Analysis:* an advertised "$49.99 oil change" plus a mandatory shop-imposed "environmental/disposal" fee is a drip-pricing risk; §3356(g) already bans generic shop-supply invoice charges.
- **CLRA §1770(a)(15)–(16):** misrepresenting that repair is needed, or that work was supplied as promised → private right of action.
- **"Certified" used vehicles — Vehicle Code §11713.18** (not §11950, which concerns buy-here-pay-here market-value labels). A dealer may not call a used car "certified" if it has odometer issues, lemon or salvage brand, substantial impact, fire, flood or frame damage, or is sold as-is/with warranty disclaimer. Buyer must get a **completed inspection report listing every component inspected** before sale.
- **Recording phone authorizations:** CA requires **all-party consent** (Penal Code §632; cell/cordless §632.7). Penalties up to $2,500/violation (more for repeats); civil claim $5,000/violation or 3× damages (§637.2); unlawful recordings generally inadmissible. Shops recording approval calls need an up-front disclosure; written notes under §3353.1(c) are required either way. Consumers: don't secretly record; take notes.
- **CCPA/CPRA:** applies to shops meeting a threshold, e.g., gross revenue > **$26,625,000** (CPI-adjusted, eff. Jan 1, 2025). DVI photos, VINs, telematics, contact data are personal information. New regs (risk assessments, cybersecurity audits, ADMT) finalized Sept 2025; some provisions eff. Jan 1, 2026; phase-in dates [UNVERIFIED].
- **Garage/mechanic's lien (Civil Code §3068 et seq.):** possessory lien for compensation **legally owed** for repairs and storage; attaches when written statement of charges presented or 15 days after completion, whichever earlier. With owner consent, shop must apply for lien sale or sue within 30 days or lien extinguished. Also extinguished if shop refuses legal owner an inspection or won't produce WO/invoice and authorization within 10 days of demand. **Caps (§3068(c)(1)):** if the work or storage was requested by **someone other than the legal owner or lessor**, the lien is invalid above **$1,500** for work and **$1,025** for storage (**$1,250** if a §3071 lien-sale application, for vehicles valued over $4,000, is filed within 30 days of storage starting), unless the shop gave the legal owner/lessor prior written notice and got written consent. So the caps bite on financed or leased cars (lender/lessor is legal owner, driver ordered work), not on an owner with clear title. They limit the lien, not the debt. Storage after 60 days invalid without Veh. Code §§10650/10652 notices. (Mirror text; DMV manual agrees on amounts: https://dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook/vehicle-industry-registration-procedures-manual-2/lien-sales-abandoned-abated-vehicles/liens/) BAR: lien sale only for authorized charges with compliant invoices. *Analysis:* amounts above the authorized estimate are arguably not "legally owed." AB 987 (2025) adds holiday storage overcharges and certain emergency tows to presumptively unreasonable fees (Veh. Code §§10652.5, 22524.5).

## 14) Complaint process

- Resolve with shop first → file with BAR online (also mail, phone, in person per 2020 article; anonymous possible). Representative contacts consumer in **7–10 business days**, gathers estimates, invoices, photos, may inspect the vehicle, mediates.
- Outcomes: refund, bill adjustment, no-charge re-repair; BAR negotiates ~$5M/yr in relief.
- Not covered: repairs outside CA, rental cars, boats, heavy-duty commercial vehicles and trailers.
- A complaint is not a finding of violation; violations may lead to education, office conference, citation, or investigation/Accusation.
- Complaint reports go in the shop's master file and **can be subpoenaed for small claims or civil court**.
- No BAR authority found to *order* restitution through mediation; restitution is a citation mitigating factor (§3394.51) and possible probation term. Binding recovery via small claims or civil court (CLRA, UCL) [UNVERIFIED as official BAR statement]. No filing deadline published.
- Escalation path: shop management → BAR complaint → small claims or CLRA demand; collision → BAR free collision inspection; insurer issues → CDI. Evidence: estimates, revised estimates, timestamped texts/emails/app approvals, invoice, returned parts, photos, DVI report, independent shop's measurements, VIR or VSSI report.

## 17) Inspection-finding checks

**For each MPI/DVI finding:**
- **Each finding is a "statement."** Known/should-have-known false → §9884.7(a)(1); with intent or gross negligence → fraud (a)(4),(e); CLRA §1770(a)(15). Audit question: *what measured evidence supports this?*
- **Measurements, not adjectives:** pad/shoe mm or 32nds; rotor thickness vs cast minimum; drum diameter vs maximum; tread in 32nds at several points; battery test printout (CCA or state of health); fluid test-strip/meter readings; scan-tool DTC and freeze-frame. **Ball-joint invoice without measured wear AND maker's max allowable wear is a direct regulatory defect (§3360.2).**
- **VSSI minimums as a floor, not a replacement trigger:** pads above service limit (or 1/32 in bonded if no spec), rotors above minimum, tires above 2/32 in would *pass* CA's state safety inspection. A "red/fail/unsafe" label above those limits needs justification (wear rate, manufacturer's earlier replace spec). "Yellow/monitor" is defensible; calling it *required* or *unsafe* invites (a)(1) and (a)(8) scrutiny. Note CA's 1/32 in bonded-lining floor is lower than the 2/32 in most inspection states use.
- **A/C "inspection"/"performance check"** used or billed → §3366 16-point standard; invoice must record pressures and vent temperature.
- **Transmission:** pre-removal inspection and code retrieval done (§3361.1)? "Rebuilt/reman" backed by defined work? Cheaper external fix offered?
- **Smog-driven:** fail = condition, not part. Ask for diagnostic path (§3340.41(d)), VIR, emissions warranty or CAP eligibility. Merely incomplete readiness monitors may need a drive cycle, not parts.
- **Flush/service packages:** compare to owner's-manual schedule (BAR tells owners to follow the manual). Ads/menus creating a false impression services are needed are misleading (§3372.1).
- **Open recalls:** VSSI fails open recalls; recall repairs free at dealers. Paid work on a recalled component without flagging the recall is a red flag.
- **Photos:** not required, but photos sent for approval are records (3 yr, unique ID).

**Consumer notes:** ask for the written estimate and ask **now, at the WO stage,** for replaced parts back; ask for measurements and photos for each red item; refuse vague lines; you may decline and take the car (shop must reassemble within stated max time after teardown); a shop can hold the car only for charges legally owed — request WO, authorizations and invoice in writing.

**Shop-owner (QA) notes:** sample ROs monthly against A1–A15; cross-check DVI red items against measured values and declined-work/sell-through rates per tech and advisor (outliers = Sears-pattern risk); review pay plans so findings require evidence; force numeric fields for brakes, tires, ball joints, batteries, A/C; confirm text-approval platform captures date, time, approver name, phone/email, links to RO #, shows a revised estimate with **new total** before approval, and exports 3 years; remove "shop supplies"/"misc." lines; itemize hazardous-waste fee with EPA ID; make advertised prices all-in; ARD sign posted, mobile techs carry sign copies, online listings show name/ARD #/phone; adopt July 2025 teardown, third-party and towing templates. If cited: respond fast; restitution and cooperation reduce penalties.
